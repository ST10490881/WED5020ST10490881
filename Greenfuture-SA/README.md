## Project Overview

GreenFuture SA is a fictional community-based non-profit organisation which was founded in 2012 by locals to locals in Cape Town, South Africa.

Through community sustainability projects, GreenFuture SA aims to make communities waste conscious and environmental custodians. To this end, it will work closely with communities to help them understand and change their ways of lifestyle that lead to unsustainable and dirty environment.

GreenFuture SA website will serve many purposes apart from providing information about GreenFuture SA. For instance, visitors will also be able to get the latest updates on GreenFuture SA's environmental projects through news, blog and video section of the site as well as they will be given opportunity of volunteering their help in the form of volunteering or sponsorship via an enquire form.
---

## Website Goals and Objectives

The central purpose of the GreenFuture SA website is to:

- Bring people's attention to environmental issues.

- Teach visitors how to live sustainably.

- Encourage reducing waste and protecting the environment.

- Highlight GreenFuture SA projects and initiatives.

- Invite visitors to become volunteers.

- Enable organisations to get in touch about sponsorship opportunities.

- Let visitors get in touch with the organisation.

- Build an online presence for GreenFuture SA that is professional in nature.
---

## Target Audience

The site was created for - communities and neighbourhoods that live nearby

- Schools and educational institutions - people who want to lead an environmentally friendly lifestyle

- Volunteers who love helping out the environment - companies who are interested in green business

- People who want to be environmental activists and donate - Potential sponsors and donors

## Key Features and Functionality

The Part 1 website contains the following pages:

### Home
Provides an introduction to GreenFuture SA and highlights the organisation's environmental goals.

### About Us
Provides information about the organisation, including its background, mission, vision and target audience.

### Projects
Provides information about GreenFuture SA's environmental initiatives, including waste minimisation, community clean-ups, conservation and environmental education.

### Enquiry
Provides visitors with opportunities to enquire about volunteering and sponsorship.

### Contact
Provides contact information, multiple organisation locations and a contact enquiry form.
