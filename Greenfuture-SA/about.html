About GreenFuture SA

GreenFuture SA is a fictional community-based non-profit organisation established in 2012 in Cape Town, South Africa.

The organisation focuses on community sustainability, waste minimisation and environmental conservation. GreenFuture SA aims to educate and encourage communities to take responsibility for creating cleaner and greener environments.

Our Mission

Our mission is to educate and empower South African communities to adopt sustainable lifestyles and take practical action to protect the environment.

Our Vision

Our vision is a world in which every community takes responsibility for creating a cleaner, greener and more sustainable environment.

Who We Serve

  • Local communities and neighbourhoods
  • Schools and educational institutions
  • Individuals interested in sustainable living
  • Businesses interested in environmental initiatives
  • Volunteers and community supporters
  • Potential sponsors and donors
