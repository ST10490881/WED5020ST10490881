Creating a Cleaner and Greener Future

Welcome to GreenFuture SA, a community-based non-profit organisation dedicated to promoting environmental sustainability, waste minimisation and conservation across South Africa.

Who We Are

Established in 2012 in Cape Town, GreenFuture SA works to educate and empower communities to adopt sustainable lifestyles and take action to protect the environment.

Our Goals

  • Raise awareness about environmental problems.
  • Promote sustainable living.
  • Encourage waste minimisation.
  • Support environmental conservation.
  • Encourage community participation.
